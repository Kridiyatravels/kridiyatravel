# Citrus Gold

A bold and radiant theme dominated by vibrant orange and rich yellow-gold tones. Energetic, warm, and highly visible — perfect for making a strong impression.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----------|-----|-------|
| Primary | **Vivid Orange** | `#f97316` | Headlines, primary buttons, key highlights |
| Secondary | **Golden Yellow** | `#fbbf24` | Subheadings, badges, icons, hover states |
| Accent | **Amber Gold** | `#d97706` | Borders, dividers, subtle emphasis |
| Light | **Cream** | `#fff7ed` | Page/slide backgrounds, cards |
| Dark | **Deep Bronze** | `#431407` | Body text, dark sections, contrast elements |

## Typography

- **Headers**: Montserrat Bold (or Poppins Bold) — clean, modern, high impact
- **Body Text**: Inter (or Open Sans) — highly readable, neutral, pairs well with bold headers
- **Accent / Quotes**: Playfair Display Italic — adds elegance for pull quotes or special callouts

## Usage Patterns

| Element | Background | Text | Notes |
|---------|-----------|------|-------|
| Hero Section | Deep Bronze `#431407` | Golden Yellow `#fbbf24` | High contrast, dramatic |
| Content Cards | Cream `#fff7ed` | Deep Bronze `#431407` | Clean, readable |
| Primary Button | Vivid Orange `#f97316` | White `#ffffff` | Strong CTA |
| Secondary Button | Transparent | Vivid Orange `#f97316` | Bordered, outlined |
| Highlight Box | Yellow 200 `#fef08a` | Deep Bronze `#431407` | Callout, tip boxes |
| Dark Section | Deep Bronze `#431407` | Cream `#fff7ed` | Footer, dark features |

## Gradients

- **Sunrise**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #f97316 0%, #fbbf24 100%)` — Orange to Gold
- **Golden Glow**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #fb923c 0%, #fde047 100%)` — Soft orange to bright yellow
- **Bronze Depth**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #431407 0%, #9a3412 100%)` — Dark bronze to burnt orange

## Best Used For

- Energy brands, sports, fitness
- Food & beverage (juice, citrus, spice)
- Sales and promotional campaigns
- Autumn/fall-themed content
- Youth and entertainment brands
- Warning/high-visibility materials

## Accessibility Notes

- Vivid Orange `#f97316` on White `#ffffff` = **4.5:1** (passes WCAG AA for normal text)
- Golden Yellow `#fbbf24` on Deep Bronze `#431407` = **7.8:1** (passes WCAG AAA)
- Always use Deep Bronze `#431407` (not pure black) for text on light backgrounds — softer and on-theme
