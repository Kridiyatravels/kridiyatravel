Hotel booking FAQs

Why enquire instead of booking instantly online?

Because a human beats an algorithm on price and fit. We hold trade rates with major chains, know which "4★" hotels are really 3★, and bundle breakfast, transfers and late checkout that websites charge extra for.

Do you handle hotel bookings for visa applications?

Yes — confirmed, verifiable hotel bookings suitable for Schengen, UK and US visa files, matched exactly to your travel dates.

Can I pay at the hotel?

Depends on the rate. We'll always show you both options where available: a lower prepaid rate and a flexible pay-at-hotel rate.
