About Kridiya

We started because travel should feel effortless, not stressful.

Our story

Kridiya Travel and Tourism was founded in Ras Al Khaimah with a single conviction: that everyone deserves expert travel guidance, not just those with corporate accounts or luxury budgets.

From our office in RAK, we plan holidays for families, honeymoons for newlyweds, sacred Umrah journeys for pilgrims, and conference trips for businesses across the UAE. Every booking is handled personally. Every question answered the same day.

We are not a platform. We are a team of people who love travel, who have been to many of the places we recommend, and who treat your trip with the same care we would give our own.

What makes us different

Personalised service
We do not sell template packages. Your trip is built from your dates, your budget, your preferences, and adjusted until it is right.
Transparent fees
Every cost is explained before you confirm. Government fees, service charges, airline taxes, all itemised. No surprises at checkout.
Same-day response
We respond to every WhatsApp message within two hours during business hours. Urgent requests outside hours are handled by our on-call team.
End-to-end support
We do not disappear after you book. We are reachable during your trip if anything changes, whether it is a flight delay or a hotel issue.
