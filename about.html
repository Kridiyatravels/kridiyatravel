Who we are

We started Kridiya Travel because we watched too many people overpay for tickets, lose money on rigid bookings, and get lost in visa paperwork. Booking websites are fine — until your plans change, your flight cancels, or your application needs a human who cares.

That's where we live. Our team quotes flights across 40+ airlines, builds holidays we've actually experienced, files visa applications that get approved, and answers the phone at hours no website ever will. From a family flying home to Kerala for a wedding, to a honeymoon in the Maldives, to a group Umrah departure — we handle it personally, end to end.

We're proudly based in Ras Al Khaimah and serve travellers across the UAE and the Gulf, in English, Hindi, Malayalam and Arabic.
