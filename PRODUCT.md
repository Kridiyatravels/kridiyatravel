# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Travelers in the UAE and the wider Gulf (residents, expats from India/Pakistan/Philippines, and tourists) planning flights, hotels, holiday packages, visas, and Umrah trips. They arrive on mobile or desktop with a concrete task: search a fare, request a package quote, apply for a visa, or reach the agency on WhatsApp. Trust and speed of contact matter more than browsing.

## Product Purpose

The official website of **Kridiya Travel and Tourism FZ-LLC** (Ras Al Khaimah, UAE) — an online travel agency in the mold of Akbar Travels. Every surface converts to an enquiry: flight/hotel/holiday/visa searches produce structured enquiries emailed via FormSubmit to enquiry@kridiyatravel.com, the contact form mails contact@kridiyatravel.com, and WhatsApp/call (+971 50 941 3873) is one tap away everywhere. Customers can register/log in (client-side account system) and see their enquiry history. Success = a visitor completes an enquiry or opens WhatsApp within a minute.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trustworthy, energetic. Slogan: **"Your Journey, Our Passion."** Visual language mirrors classic Gulf OTA portals (Akbar Travels): light page, structural blue for tabs/links/bands, orange CTAs and prices, the KD logo's orange/gold reserved for brand marks. Dense and functional, not an editorial landing page.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS gradient-hero landing pages.
- Cream/beige "AI default" body backgrounds.
- Fake booking engines that pretend to show live fares — every result is honestly an enquiry/quote flow.
- Cluttered banner-farm OTA pages (popups, blinking deals).

## Design Principles

1. **Search-first** — the booking widget is the hero on every travel page; content supports it.
2. **One tap to a human** — WhatsApp, call, and email visible on every screen.
3. **Honest enquiry flow** — no fake prices; searches become structured quote requests.
4. **Earned familiarity** — standard OTA affordances (tabs, autocomplete, steppers) done precisely.
5. **Mobile is the primary device** — every flow tested at 375px.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text), full keyboard operability on the search widgets and autocomplete, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all animation, form errors announced inline with text (not color alone).
