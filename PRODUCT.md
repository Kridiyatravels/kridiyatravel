# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UAE and RAK-based travellers, families, couples, pilgrims, and corporate travel planners who want a human travel agency to compare options, answer questions quickly, and move enquiries into WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Kridiya Travel & Tourism presents flights, holiday packages, honeymoon trips, cruises, visa help, Umrah and Hajj travel, and corporate travel services. Success means visitors quickly understand the service range, find a relevant travel idea, and contact the team by WhatsApp, phone, or enquiry form.

## Brand Personality

Warm, trusted, curated, and responsive. The site should feel like a polished travel concierge: premium enough to inspire confidence, direct enough to make enquiry easy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic template travel pages, empty image placeholders, unclear CTA paths, over-decorated effects, and pages that hide the agency contact path behind too many steps.

## Design Principles

Show destinations with real imagery wherever possible.
Keep WhatsApp and calling routes obvious.
Preserve the existing Kridiya navy, fire orange, gold, and warm sand identity.
Make sections complete and scannable on both desktop and mobile.
Prefer simple, dependable static interactions over fragile page-specific scripts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, readable type sizes, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support for decorative animation.
