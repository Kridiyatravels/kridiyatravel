# Kridiya Illustration Prompt System

## Shared wide-scene prompt

Create a fully original `1600×700` premium monoline editorial explainer
illustration for Kridiya Travel and Tours, a UAE travel agency. Use thin
graphite/charcoal-grey outlines, mostly white space, clean geometric travel
objects, minimal warm human characters, and small soft flat fills only.
Palette: saffron orange `#F2820C`, golden yellow `#F6C445`, warm cream, white,
deep brown, and tiny muted blue or green accents only where useful. Keep the
composition spacious, calm, polished, and professional.

Output on a transparent background. Include no readable text, logos,
watermarks, gradients, heavy shadows, blur, 3D objects, mascot styling,
childish cartoon styling, glossy icon-pack styling, random stickers, dark or
green backgrounds, black borders, video bars, code symbols, technology logos,
or rough-sketch linework.

## Scene matrix

- Homepage: travel advisor desk with UAE family, route line, ticket, passport,
  luggage, and subtle airport geometry.
- Flights: airport traveller, boarding pass, route map, aircraft trail, orange
  rolling suitcase, and thin terminal geometry.
- Hotels: receptionist and guest, bed/resort cue, luggage, palm detail, and
  blank booking confirmation card.
- Holidays: couple or family planning at a desk with camera, beach hat,
  itinerary, route map, pins, and resort cues.
- Umrah: respectful family travel preparation with luggage, passport folder,
  crescent, and restrained Makkah/Madinah-inspired travel geometry.
- Cruise: cruise ship, traveller with blank itinerary, cabin card, wave line,
  route, port cue, and small luggage.
- Visa: passport, checklist, approval stamp/checkmark, envelope, appointment
  calendar, and document folder arranged as a professional desk scene.
- Business travel: corporate traveller with laptop at an airport lounge,
  calendar, boarding pass, company travel folder, and luggage.
- About: consultant team at a desk with map wall, phone, laptop, and documents.
- Contact: headset advisor with blank message bubbles, phone, location pin,
  and document folder.
- Account: laptop and phone portal scene with shield/checkmark, passport
  wallet, booking folder, and subtle route line.

## Mini spot prompt

Create one centered `512×512` transparent vector spot illustration in the same
Kridiya monoline system. Use a rounded `#5B554F` outline, mostly white or
`#FFF6E7` interiors, and restrained `#F2820C`, `#F6C445`, or deep-brown fills.
Keep the silhouette clean and leave generous transparent margins. No text,
logo, watermark, background panel, gradient, shadow, 3D, or rough SVG look.
