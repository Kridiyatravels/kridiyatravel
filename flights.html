Flight booking FAQs

How do I actually book after searching?

Run your search above, then send it to us by email or WhatsApp with one tap. A travel expert replies with the exact bookable fare. Once you approve, pay by card payment link or UAE bank transfer; we then issue the ticket and email it to you.

How do your fares compare with booking websites?

We check suitable published and available consolidated options for your route. Prices and conditions vary, so we provide the available choices for you to compare before approving a booking.

Can you handle date changes and refunds?

Yes. Every ticket we issue comes with human after-sales support on the same phone number: +971 50 941 3873. We process changes, refunds and airline schedule-change claims for you.

Do you book group and corporate travel?

Absolutely - group fares for 10+ passengers, corporate accounts with monthly billing, and crew/marine fares. Email enquiry@kridiyatravel.com with your requirements.
