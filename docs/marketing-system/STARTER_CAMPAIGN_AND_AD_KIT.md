# Starter Campaign and Ad Kit

## Campaign

**Name:** One Message. A Whole Journey.
**Campaign ID:** `kt-2026-always-on-001`
**Objective:** Generate qualified UAE travel enquiries through WhatsApp and service-specific forms.
**Audience:** UAE residents planning flights, holidays, hotels, or visa-supported trips.
**Primary promise:** One responsive advisor can help compare and coordinate the practical parts of the journey.
**CTA:** Get a personalised quote.

## Offer

Send:

- destination or route;
- travel dates;
- number of travellers;
- preferred budget or travel style.

Receive:

- a personal response;
- suitable options subject to availability;
- clear inclusions, conditions, and next steps.

No price, availability, or visa outcome is guaranteed until checked and confirmed.

## A/B test

**Hypothesis:** Replacing a generic “Get a quote” message with the explicit input request—destination, dates, travellers—will reduce low-information messages and increase qualified WhatsApp enquiries.

- Control: generic CTA.
- Variant: qualification-led CTA.
- Primary metric: qualified enquiries / WhatsApp conversations.
- Guardrails: total conversations, response SLA, block/spam rate.
- Decision: run until the pre-agreed sample threshold; do not stop because of early daily variation.

## Launch checklist

- Offer and claims approved by operations.
- Landing page matches the ad.
- UTMs and campaign ID tested.
- WhatsApp prefill tested on mobile.
- Enquiry source saved in CRM.
- Staff assigned during campaign hours.
- Quote and follow-up templates ready.
- Budget and stop-loss approved.
- Privacy/consent language reviewed.
- Creative expiry date recorded.
- Test enquiry completed from ad click to CRM.
- Booking/gross-profit reporting path confirmed.

## Kill, fix, or scale

- **Kill:** misleading traffic, poor lead quality, unavailable offer, or negative contribution with sufficient data.
- **Fix:** slow response, weak qualification, landing mismatch, tracking failure, or repetitive creative.
- **Scale:** qualified lead quality, response SLA, bookings, and gross-profit contribution all meet target.
