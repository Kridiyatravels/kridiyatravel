## Phase definitions

- **Now / Phase 1:** foundation and first 30 days.
- **Phase 2:** days 31–90 after tracking and response operations work.
- **Phase 3:** months 4–12 after channel economics are proven.
- **Deferred:** only after the required product/channel exists.
- **Not applicable:** intentionally excluded from the current model.
