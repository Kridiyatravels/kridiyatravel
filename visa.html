FAQ

Common Questions

How long does a Schengen visa take?+
Typically 10–15 working days from the appointment date. We recommend applying 3–4 weeks before travel to allow for delays. We handle the full process.
What is your service fee?+
Our fees start from AED 150 for simple assistance and go up to AED 600 for complex applications. Government and embassy fees are always separate and paid directly. We are fully transparent. No surprises.
Can you guarantee visa approval?+
No agency can legally guarantee approval. That decision rests with the embassy. What we can tell you is that our thorough document preparation and review process gives your application the strongest possible chance of success.
Do you handle express or urgent applications?+
Yes, for select destinations. WhatsApp us with your travel date and we will tell you honestly whether express processing is possible and what it costs.
I have a UAE residence visa - not a passport. Can I still apply?+
Absolutely. We process visa applications for UAE residents of all nationalities. Your residence status, employment type, and travel history all help. Contact us and we will advise based on your specific situation.
